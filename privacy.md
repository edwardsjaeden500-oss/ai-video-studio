# AI Video Studio – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 August 2026**

## 1. About AI Video Studio

AI Video Studio is a desktop application for creating, editing, managing, scheduling and publishing video content.

## 2. Information Used by the Application

AI Video Studio may process information required to provide its features, including:

- Video and audio files selected by the user.
- Video descriptions, hashtags and publishing settings.
- TikTok account authorization information provided through TikTok's official authorization system.
- Technical information necessary to operate the application and its integrations.

## 3. TikTok Account Information

When a user connects a TikTok account, authorization is performed using TikTok's official authorization system.

AI Video Studio does not request or store the user's TikTok password.

The application may store authorization tokens and account identifiers required to maintain the authorized connection and perform actions requested by the user.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to provide AI Video Studio functionality, including:

- Creating and editing videos.
- Managing video files and publishing information.
- Connecting authorized TikTok accounts.
- Uploading or publishing videos requested by the user.
- Scheduling videos for publication.
- Maintaining the user's publishing workflow.

## 5. User Content

Users retain ownership of their videos, audio files and other content.

AI Video Studio processes user content only for functions requested by the user.

## 6. Sharing of Information

AI Video Studio does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to TikTok when necessary to provide TikTok integration features requested and authorized by the user.

## 7. Data Storage

AI Video Studio is primarily a locally operated desktop application.

Application data, video files and configuration information may be stored on the user's computer.

TikTok authorization credentials required for an authorized connection may also be stored locally for use by the application.

## 8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored application information and authentication credentials.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their computer and connected accounts.

## 9. Revoking TikTok Access

Users may disconnect their TikTok account from AI Video Studio where supported or revoke the application's authorization through TikTok.

After authorization is revoked, AI Video Studio will no longer be able to perform authorized TikTok actions using that connection.

## 10. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with AI Video Studio.

Locally stored application data may also be removed from the user's computer by deleting the relevant application data.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as AI Video Studio and its integrations develop.

## 12. Contact

Questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy may be directed to the developer of AI Video Studio.
